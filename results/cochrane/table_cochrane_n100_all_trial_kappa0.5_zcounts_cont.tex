\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item Continuous efficacy outcomes of randomized trials with at least 100 participants in the Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (the data of van Zwet, Schwab and Senn 2021); $z$ is the mean difference over its standard error, from the printed arm means and standard deviations; outcomes whose rounding unit in $z$-units, $\kappa = 2a/\mathrm{SE}_{\min}$, exceeds 0.5 are left out. De-rounding: None uses the printed values; Uniform draws each mean and standard deviation uniformly within its rounding cell; $s_{\min}$ rescales $z$ to the smallest standard error consistent with the printed standard deviations; $s_{\min}$ + noise also adds uniform noise at each mean's last digit. Under Uniform and $s_{\min}$ + noise, entries are averages over 200 draws, with standard deviations across draws in parentheses. Projection test: $J = 40$, $J_0 = 20$, $p = 2$, centered at 1.96. Allowance: the rounding allowance $0.13\,\overline{\kappa^6}$, times $10^3$ (Propositions 3 and 4); under $s_{\min}$ and $s_{\min}$ + noise, the projection test rejects only when $\hat B$ exceeds it. Every test clusters by trial. EKW: the smallest of the six tests' $p$-values of Elliott, Kudrin and W\"uthrich (2022), each averaged over draws as above; not itself a valid $p$-value. $p$-curve: Stouffer's test for left skew (Simonsohn, Nelson and Simmons 2014). Bold: $p$-values of at most .05, and $10^3 \hat B$ above the allowance.
