\begin{tablenotes}[flushleft]\footnotesize
\item Entries are $p$-values; bold marks $p \le .05$. Printed: the test statistics as computed from the printed coefficients and standard errors (Panel A) or the printed arm means and standard deviations (Panel B). De-rounded: every printed number drawn uniformly within its rounding interval; $p$-values averaged over 200 draws. Projection test: $J = 40$, $J_0 = 20$, centered at 1.96. Certified: the projection test under the smallest standard error consistent with the printed one (Section 5.2); its rejection is certified when $10^3 \hat B$ exceeds the rounding allowance $10^3 \times 0.13\,\overline{\kappa^6}$ (Propositions 3 and 4), shown in parentheses; bold when it does. EKW and $p$-curve: the smallest of the $p$-values of the six tests of Elliott, Kudrin and W\"uthrich (2022) and the $p$-curve test for left skew (Simonsohn, Nelson and Simmons 2014), each averaged over draws; it is not itself a valid $p$-value and overstates their evidence. The projection test, Kudrin's tests, CS1 and CS2B cluster by article (Panel A) or trial (Panel B); the other EKW tests and the $p$-curve test treat the statistics as independent. Statistics whose rounding interval exceeds half a standard error are left out ($\kappa \le 0.5$); estimates printed as zero are kept. Panel B: continuous efficacy outcomes of randomized trials with at least 100 participants. Published by 2005 and after 2005: trials by year of publication (the study's year, else the year in the trial's label); the 117 outcomes without a year are in neither. $T_\infty$'s rejections in Panel B come from $p$-values near 1: outcomes whose two arms have equal printed means (Figure~\ref{fig:cochrane_tinf_gap}). Every rounding treatment, and standard deviations across draws: Appendix Tables~\ref{tab:rounding_ekw_keepmu0_cochrane} and~\ref{tab:cochrane}.
