1. SETTINGS THAT DEFINE THE SAMPLE
  drop_zero_mu = FALSE (coefficients printed as zero kept); kappa_cap = 0.5; se_rounded_up = FALSE; drop_pvalue_t = TRUE
  kappa = a / s_min: the coefficient's rounding unit a (the finest place value among its table's coefficients, but no
  finer than its standard error's) over the smallest standard error consistent with the printed one, s - b/2.
  A t-statistic printed without a standard error has kappa = a_t, the finest place value among its article's printed
  t-statistics: the file has lost trailing zeros, so a t printed 2.00 is stored as 2. The cap applies to both kinds.
  A t-statistic with 4 or more decimals was computed by the file, not printed. Converted back, it gives a p-value with
  the number of decimals below; few decimals mean a printed p-value. Rounding a p-value is not rounding t on a lattice,
  so Propositions 3 and 4 do not cover these t-statistics: they are dropped.
