\begin{minipage}{\linewidth}\footnotesize\textit{Notes:} Rejection rates of 5\%-level tests over 500 repetitions, with no switching ($\tau = 0$: size) and when every trial switches ($\tau = 1$). Each meta-sample draws 1,500 of the 3,282 randomized trials in the Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews (Schwab's data set, as in van Zwet, Schwab and Senn 2021) with at least 2 continuous efficacy outcomes of at least 100 participants, without replacement, and reports one outcome per trial. A trial chooses among its first 8 outcomes in review order. With probability $1 - \tau$ it reports one chosen at random (its pre-specified outcome); with probability $\tau$ it reports its most significant outcome (Most significant) or, when the pre-specified outcome has $|z| \le 1.96$, the larger of it and one other outcome (Threshold). Trials select on unrounded $z$: each repetition draws the means and standard deviations uniformly within their rounding cells. Columns within each group: the analyst's treatment of rounding. None: the printed $z$. Unif.: means and standard deviations drawn uniformly within their rounding cells. $s_{\min}$: the mean difference over $SE_{\min}$, the standard error from the smallest standard deviations consistent with the printed ones. $+$noise: the same with uniform noise at each mean's last digit. Outcomes whose rounding unit in $z$-units, $\kappa = 2a/SE_{\min}$ ($a$ the unit of the means), exceeds 0.5 are left out. Projection test: $J = 40$, $J_0 = 20$, centered at 1.96, 200 bootstrap repetitions; under $s_{\min}$ and $+$noise it rejects only when its breakdown statistic $\hat B$ exceeds the rounding allowance 0.13 times the mean of $\kappa^6$ over the reported outcomes (Propositions 3 and 4), and otherwise when $p \le .05$; the row ``$p \le .05$ only'' ignores the allowance. Kudrin (2025): $T_\infty$, with 199 bootstrap repetitions, and $T_H$ and $T_H^{\mathrm{hyb}}$, with the sinc kernel and frequency cutoff 3. EKW: the tests of Elliott, Kudrin and W\"uthrich (2022), as in the application, on the two-sided $p$-values below 0.15: LCM and Fisher, both of a concave $p$-curve CDF, the density discontinuity test at .05, the binomial test on $[.04,.05]$, and the Cox--Shi histogram tests of monotonicity (CS1) and 2-monotonicity with bounds (CS2B). $p$-curve: the test for left skew of Simonsohn, Nelson and Simmons (2014), by Stouffer's method. The two ``smallest $p$-value'' rows reject when the smallest of their tests' $p$-values is at most .05; they are not size-controlled tests. Every test treats trials as independent units. Bold: a size above .069, the upper end of the 95\% Monte Carlo band around 5\%.\end{minipage}
